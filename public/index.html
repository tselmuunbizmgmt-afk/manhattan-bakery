Our story

“New York roots. Ulaanbaatar made.”

Our family spent almost a decade living in New York. Muffins and pastries were something we made and shared with the people around us long before there was a brand. The Manhattan Bakery grew from that — a way to bring the bakery culture, flavor and energy we knew in New York into a modern Ulaanbaatar experience.
